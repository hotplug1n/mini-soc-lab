# Investigation Playbook

This playbook describes the first-pass workflow for alerts generated by the lab.

## 1. Validate the alert

Confirm the source, target account, timestamp and detection rule. Check for malformed telemetry or parsing assumptions.

## 2. Establish a timeline

Group events by source and account. Determine whether failures occurred in a tight sequence and whether a successful authentication followed.

## 3. Check context

Ask:

- Is the source expected or known?
- Is the account privileged?
- Was the login time consistent with normal activity?
- Are there related events around the same timestamp?

## 4. Classify

A failed burst without a successful login may remain suspicious but contained. A successful login after repeated failures receives the highest priority in this lab because it may indicate credential compromise.

## 5. Response recommendations

Depending on the validated evidence, an analyst could recommend terminating suspicious sessions, resetting affected credentials, increasing monitoring, blocking a malicious source where appropriate, and escalating according to the incident-response process.

## 6. Evidence handling

Preserve the original log, record timestamps and detection output, avoid modifying source evidence, and document decisions so the investigation can be reproduced.

> This is a training playbook for synthetic lab data, not an operational incident-response procedure.
